How it works

From research to relationship

1. Research

Prospects that are not yet Caritas partners are collected per district with CSR evidence and sources.

2. Score

Eight scoring agents, a reputation gate and a reviewer rate each company; the result is calibrated against existing Caritas partners.

3. Act

Tiers A (approach now), B (nurture) and C (low priority) tell the team where to start.
