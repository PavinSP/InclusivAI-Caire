# Caritas Corporate Partner AI Challenge

## Objective

Caritasverband für die Diözese Würzburg e. V. is looking for philanthropy-minded companies in Lower Franconia that share its values and may be interested in financially supporting its work.

The proposed solution is an AI-powered matchmaker that identifies promising corporate partners and explains why each company is a good fit.

## Information the AI should evaluate

- Company values and purpose
- Corporate social responsibility (CSR) activities
- Philanthropic interests
- Regional presence and ties to Lower Franconia
- Previous donations, partnerships, or community engagement
- Evidence from annual reports, regional newspapers, company websites, and other credible sources

## Desired output

The prototype should answer:

> Which companies should Caritas approach, and why are they a good match?

Each recommendation should include a clear rationale and supporting evidence rather than only a numerical score.

## Available Caritas information

The challenge provides information about:

1. Caritas' geographic operating area in Lower Franconia
2. Caritas' values and principles
3. Caritas' fields of activity and services

The Excel file describes the facilities and activities. `Caritas_Werte.pdf` describes the organization's identity and values.

## Suggested matching workflow

1. Use the Caritas facility data to determine local needs and locations.
2. Translate the values document into transparent matching criteria.
3. Research companies with a regional connection.
4. Extract CSR themes, previous engagement, and relevant evidence.
5. Score or rank companies against the criteria.
6. Present the best matches with an explanation, sources, and suggested outreach angle.

## Suggested company-data fields

Company name, sector, headquarters or branches, Lower Franconia connection, CSR themes, relevant projects, philanthropic interests, previous partnerships, contact information, source URLs, evidence excerpts, value alignment, activity alignment, regional fit, confidence, and recommended outreach message.
