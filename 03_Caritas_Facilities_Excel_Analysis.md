# Analysis of `Caritas_Einrichtungen.xlsx`

## Purpose of the spreadsheet

This workbook is not a list of companies. It is a directory of Caritas facilities and services. It supplies the challenge with geographic coverage and a practical view of Caritas' fields of activity.

## Structure

- One worksheet: `Sheet`
- 980 facility records, excluding the header row
- 103 facility or service types
- 226 towns
- 223 postal codes
- Seven columns

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `Einrichtungsart` | Type of service or facility |
| `Einrichtungsname 1` | Main facility or organization name |
| `Einrichtungsname 2` | Branch, department, or more specific location |
| `Straße` | Street address |
| `PLZ` | German postal code |
| `Ort` | Town or municipality |
| `Ortsteil` | District or neighborhood |

## Main activity categories

- 473 kindergarten or day-care facilities
- 46 outpatient care services
- 36 elderly day-care facilities
- 26 schools or educational facilities for children with sensory or speech disabilities
- 25 full residential elderly-care facilities
- 25 school-age childcare facilities
- 22 nurseries or crèches
- 21 general social counselling services
- 14 migration, refugee, and asylum-procedure counselling services

## Main locations

The most represented towns are Würzburg (138 facilities), Aschaffenburg (62), Schweinfurt (45), Bad Kissingen (25), and Haßfurt (24).

## How it supports the AI matchmaker

The sheet can help determine:

1. Whether a company operates near Caritas facilities.
2. Which Caritas services are relevant in that area.
3. Which social issues could align with a company's CSR strategy.
4. Whether a company has a meaningful regional connection.

For example, a company with a major presence in Würzburg and a youth-development CSR program could be matched with Caritas' many childcare and education facilities there.

## Data-quality observations

- 247 records have no second facility name.
- 765 records have no district or neighborhood.
- 2 records have no street address.
- 36 exact duplicate rows appear to exist.
- The workbook contains no company names, CSR information, values, donation history, or contact data.

## Interpretation

This workbook should be treated as the Caritas-side reference dataset. A second dataset containing potential companies and evidence about them is required before meaningful partner rankings can be produced.
